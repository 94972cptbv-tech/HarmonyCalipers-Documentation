## Request help or report a defect

Contact **Harmony Calipers Support** at [harmonycal.support@icloud.com](mailto:harmonycal.support@icloud.com).

When requesting help, include:

- Harmony Calipers version and build number
- iPhone or iPad model, and iOS/iPadOS version
- Relevant instrument, tuning, tonal center, and chord/progression
- The exact sequence of taps and what happened
- Screenshots when useful, particularly for note placement or export clipping

Please do not send sensitive personal data in screenshots or attachments.

## What this support page covers

Help with progression selection, the Harmony Wheel, custom progressions, Song Builder, piano and fretted charts, and image/PDF exports. Report suspected incorrect pitches, staff notation, or chord shapes with specific details so they can be reproduced and tested.
