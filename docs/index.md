# Harmony Calipers

**Explore harmony. Build progressions. Arrange songs. Play them on your instrument.**

Harmony Calipers is a visual songwriting companion for iPhone and iPad. Its Circle of Fifths-based harmony wheel helps you explore relationships between chords. Build your own progressions, organize song sections, see instrument voicings, and export readable charts and PDF song packs.

The app is intended to complement a real instrument. It does not need to replace a piano, guitar, mandolin, ukulele, or banjo.

## What you can do

**Explore:** choose a tonal center, relationship overlay, and suggested harmonic progression.

**Customize:** edit chord names and review Roman-numeral analysis before using a progression.

**Arrange:** collect Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Intro, Interlude, Break, and Outro sections into a song form.

**Play:** inspect chord charts on Piano, Guitar, Mandolin, Mandola, Banjo, Ukulele, or Baritone Ukulele.

**Share:** create modern or traditional progression sheets, section sheets, and full-song PDF packs.

## A note about musical interpretation

Roman numerals describe harmonic roles within a tonal context; the wheel's visual distance is a different kind of relationship. Suggestions are starting points for your musical judgment. For uncertainty, consult [Harmony Concepts](reference/harmony-concepts.md).
