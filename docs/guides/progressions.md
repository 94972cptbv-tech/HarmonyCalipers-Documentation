# Suggested and Custom Progressions

## Suggested Progressions

Harmony Calipers presents progression ideas informed by key, mode, harmony type, and wheel relationship. A colored badge describes the general character of the result. [Learn the color key](../reference/harmony-concepts.md).

Select a card to see its harmonic path on the wheel. You can inspect the card, use it in **Instrument View**, or copy it into **Custom Progression**.

## Customize a suggestion

1. Tap **Edit as Custom** on a suggested progression.
2. Rename the progression and edit the chord sequence.
3. Check the tonal center, mode, and harmony type.
4. Tap **Validate** to see the analysis and Roman numerals.
5. Choose **Use in Instrument View**, **Save Progression**, or **Add Section to Song**.

Example in C major:

| Chords | Roman numerals | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C → G → Am → F | I → V → vi → IV | Diatonic pop loop |
| Dm7 → G7 → Cmaj7 | ii7 → V7 → Imaj7 | Functional ii–V–I |
| C → F → Fm → C | I → IV → iv → I | Borrowed minor iv |

A chord sequence may use borrowed or chromatic color without being a conventional ii–V–I. Validation should reflect that distinction.

## Saved Progressions

Save progressions you want to reuse. You can load them back into Custom Progression, preview them on an instrument, or add them to a song set. Saved progression analysis may be recalculated when loaded.

## Working with musical uncertainty

A label such as **Caliper-derived** describes the way an idea was suggested; it does not certify functional resolution. Use the chord and Roman-numeral display together, and listen or play to evaluate the result.
