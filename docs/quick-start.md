# Quick Start

This walkthrough begins with a four-chord progression and ends with a saved song section.

## 1. Choose a tonal center

Open Harmony Calipers and select **C Major**. The **Harmony Wheel** shows how major and relative-minor areas connect around the Circle of Fifths. Explore a relationship overlay if you want a different lens on the same tonal center.

## 2. Select a suggestion

Scroll to **Suggested Progressions** and choose a card. For a familiar example, look for a progression such as **C → G → Am → F** (*I → V → vi → IV* in C major).

The selected card is highlighted. Use **View on Wheel** to return to the wheel and examine its path. The selected progression title near the wheel can take you back to its card.

## 3. Edit as Custom

Choose **Edit as Custom**. Give the progression a title and change chords if desired. Run **Validate** to review the harmonic explanation and Roman numerals. A color-based progression should not automatically be treated as a functional cadence; see [Harmony Concepts](reference/harmony-concepts.md).

## 4. Add a song section

Choose a section type, such as **Verse**, and select **Add Section to Song**. Open **Song Builder** to edit the section title, add another progression for a **Chorus**, and reorder the sections.

## 5. Preview instrument charts

In **Instrument View**, choose **Piano** or a supported fretted instrument. Piano provides **Keyboard**, **Staff**, and **Both** views. Step through the progression to inspect each chord.

## 6. Save and export

Save the song set. Use the available **Export** actions to create a chart image or a full-song PDF pack. When Piano **Both** charts cannot fit on one page, the export may continue across additional pages rather than omitting the remaining chords.

## Tips

- Revisit the original progression by selecting its name.
- Use [Saved Progressions](guides/progressions.md) to reuse harmonic ideas.
- Use [Song Builder](guides/song-builder.md) to organize multiple sections into a complete song.
