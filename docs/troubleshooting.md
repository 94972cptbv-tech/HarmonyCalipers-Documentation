# FAQ and Troubleshooting

## Why does a progression show a purple badge?

Purple identifies **Caliper-derived** harmony: a suggestion based on wheel relationships or harmonic color. It does not represent a literal purple note or key. See [Harmony Concepts](reference/harmony-concepts.md).

## How do I return to the selected progression?

Use the tappable progression title near the wheel to return to its suggestion card. When a suggestion is selected farther down the screen, **View on Wheel** takes you back to the wheel.

## Why do Piano exports have extra pages?

**Staff + Keyboard** requires more vertical space than Keyboard alone. The exporter paginates the chord charts rather than omitting later chords. Review all exported pages.

## Why does a chord look different on mandola and mandolin?

They have different tunings; equal fret shapes do not produce equal chords. Always use the selected instrument's own chart.

## Why does a chart show a rootless or partial voicing?

Some supported voicings omit the root or other chord tones. Review the note list and helper text, and confirm unusual shapes on the instrument.

## I can't find a saved song

Open Song Builder's saved-song area and check the saved title. Saved sets are local app data; a chart you exported separately may still be in Files or another destination you selected.

## The PDF seems to omit chords

Check subsequent section/continuation pages and confirm the selected Piano display mode. If chord charts are genuinely missing, report the progression, device, and app version through [Support](support.md).

## My chord diagram or notation is wrong

Musical accuracy matters. Note the instrument/tuning, chord name, selected key, visible notes, and screenshot. Send this information to [Support](support.md). Avoid relying on a potentially incorrect chart during a performance.
